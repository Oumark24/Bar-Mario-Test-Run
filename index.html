A Neighborhood Place

To eat, drink & stay a while.

Tucked into the waterfront blocks of Red Hook, Bar Mario keeps its kitchen close to Piedmont and Tuscany — handmade pastas, wood-fired warmth, and a room built for lingering over a second glass of wine. Come for dinner, stay for the negroni by the window.

The Space

Small room, big warmth.

Dark wood, low light and close tables — Bar Mario is built to feel like a neighborhood find, not a destination restaurant. Colorful seating and a few art-deco touches soften the room without dressing it up too much. It's the kind of place where a quick glass turns into dinner.
