A Neighborhood Place

To eat, drink & stay a while.

Tucked into the waterfront blocks of Red Hook, Bar Mario keeps its kitchen close to Piedmont and Tuscany — handmade pastas, wood-fired warmth, and a room built for lingering over a good drink.
